- **Lightning: LMA source heights and lightning jumps**: for Lightning Mapping Array files the Lightning panel now shows how many sources and flash initiations lie in each height bin (km above mean sea level, with the median and the most common height) and marks lightning jumps (the "2 sigma" algorithm of Schultz et al. 2009) on the flash-rate chart; both are computed by the radarx library. It says that a distribution of located sources depends on the range from the array, that the jump needs 14 minutes of data, and that charge layers are not inferred. See {doc}`LIGHTNING`.
