- **Probabilistic rainfall.** `--ensemble N` (and the *Probabilistic rainfall* block of the Rainfall panel) builds an ensemble of the rainfall estimate under an assumed multiplicative, spatially correlated error model and gives the mean, the spread, percentiles and the probability of reaching chosen depths, per cell and per basin. The error parameters come from your gauges when you give some; otherwise they come from the estimate's own uncertainty and from defaults that are marked as assumed. Seeded and reproducible, with the run record in the output, and always labelled as an ensemble under an assumed model; with 30 or more gauges the probabilities are checked against them (Brier skill and reliability slope), otherwise the output says they are not validated. See {doc}`HYDROLOGY`.
