- **Nowcast:** `rxd nowcast` extrapolates gridded radar frames 5 to 120 minutes ahead (dense optical-flow motion, semi-Lagrangian advection, Lagrangian persistence: no growth, decay or initiation), with an ensemble from the verified motion error of the method, probabilities of exceeding thresholds, a position-uncertainty radius per lead, CF NetCDF outputs with the full run record, and `--verify`, which scores a sequence against what was observed later next to Eulerian persistence. The engine answers the `nowcast_run` message for a future panel. The method was verified on eight public NEXRAD cases that were not used to tune it (`python/benchmarks/nowcast_verify.py`): it beats persistence up to about 45 minutes for moving convection, not for stationary or rotating rain patterns, and nowhere beyond an hour; the page says so with intervals. A new provenance class **FORECAST** (chip `FCST`) carries the method, the lead time, a measured skill statement and the words "experimental guidance, not an official forecast" ({doc}`NOWCAST`, {doc}`SCIENCE`).
