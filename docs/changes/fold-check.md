- **Dealiasing is now checked, always.** The pipeline step, the QC panel's velocity dealiasing and the *Velocity Dealiasing* module test every result for a sweep that sits in the wrong multiple of two Nyquist velocities (a range-ring test with a uniform-wind VAD fit, Browning and Wexler 1968), write the verdict into the run record, and warn when the whole sweep is off or when parts of it sit in different intervals. The data are not changed and the default *Fold reference* stays `none`: on 133 Doppler cuts of 12 NEXRAD volumes no whole-sweep offset was found in the QC panel's method, so the evidence did not justify changing it ({doc}`PIPELINES`).
- **Velocity dealiasing in the QC panel uses radarx when it is installed** (new *Unfolding engine* option: auto, builtin, radarx); without radarx the built-in method is used as before. For a uniform 25 m/s wind seen with a Nyquist velocity of 10 m/s radarx is exact where the built-in method is one interval off ({doc}`PIPELINES`).
- **Impossible velocities are removed after dealiasing.** A gate beyond 150 m/s (or five Nyquist velocities) is set to missing, counted in the run record and warned about, so a rare unfolding fault of the radarx region method (velocities of hundreds of thousands of m/s on a few gates) never reaches your results ({doc}`PIPELINES`).
