- **New Lightning layer (observed)**: open GOES GLM Level 2 files or Lightning Mapping Array source files from your disk and see the flashes over the radar, in 2D and in 3D. Dots are drawn at their measured time (and, for the array, height), joined in time order by flash, brighter for stronger and fading with age; the time window follows the volume time. A panel gives the flash rate per minute and the number of sources, hatches the time the files do not cover (no data is never drawn as no lightning), and shows the GLM parallax as an uncertainty (or corrects it to a cloud-top height you choose, an assumption it states). LMA sources are linked into flashes with the space-time metric of Fuchs et al. (2016), thresholds in the panel. The same is available as `rxd lightning`. See {doc}`LIGHTNING`.
