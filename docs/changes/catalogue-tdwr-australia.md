- **Airport radars and Australian radars on the radar network map:** the map now shows the 47 US Terminal Doppler Weather Radars (TDWR; filter by *TDWR*; each card links to NOAA's free archive, and a TDWR file you download opens with *Open*, tested with one Denver volume) and the Bureau of Meteorology's radars as 82 sites with their band, Doppler and dual-polarisation flags, in-service dates, status and a link to each radar's Bureau page. The Australian rows replace 62 of the 66 earlier ones that were the same radars, and the archive's licence is now shown correctly as CC BY 4.0 (it was wrongly shown as non-commercial). The app still cannot download TDWR or Australian volumes itself, and Australian volume access was checked for one radar only; the card says so. South African radar names were tidied. {doc}`RADAR_MAP` has the sources and limits.
