- **Use the app's screen on data that stays on a cluster or server:** the new command `rxd serve` starts the engine on the cluster, listening on that computer only, with a new random token for every run; you forward its port with `ssh -L` and connect the app in *Settings > Engine* with the address and the token. The status bar says **REMOTE ENGINE** while it is in use and *Use the built-in engine* goes back. Only one app can connect at a time, the token is never saved or logged, the engine stops by itself after 120 minutes without use, and your account is still checked on both sides. {doc}`cluster` has the steps for Linux, macOS and Windows, batch jobs and the security notes; it also says what was not tested (a real cluster, Windows).
