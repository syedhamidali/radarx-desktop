- **Nowcast panel:** a new **Nowcast** panel (and two command-palette entries) makes the forecast from the open radar files, the live map's recent loop or a gridded frames file: lead time up to 120 minutes, step, 0 to 8 ensemble members, progress and Cancel. The result is drawn on the map with a lead-time slider, the nowcast and the probability of exceeding a threshold, and the position uncertainty of every lead. Every frame, on the map and in every saved picture or file, carries the **FCST** chip, the origin and valid time, the lead, the skill statement of the method and the words "experimental guidance, not an official forecast"; the panel states the measured limits ({doc}`NOWCAST`).
