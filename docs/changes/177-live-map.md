- **Live map and default radars:** choose one or several NEXRAD radars on a map (Settings › Data › Live map, or the first press of the new **Live** button; search by id, name or state; an opt-in *use my location*), and the live map shows their newest volumes merged into one composite on the map, updating every 2 to 15 minutes with pause, a LIVE status line that states each radar's scan time and age or why it did not answer, and a recent loop of the last 12 composites. The first start asks twice, for the listings and then for the exact sizes of the downloads; the engine keeps one volume per radar; the composite is labelled retrieved ({doc}`LIVE`).
