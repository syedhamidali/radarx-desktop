- **Rotation tracks.** A new module, *Rotation Tracks (radarx)*, follows low-level rotation over a sequence of volumes of one radar: a swath of the largest azimuthal shear, with the rotation couplets linked into tracks, the gaps between volumes and the limits of range-dependent resolution shown with the result. It is a retrieval, never a tornado warning or a damage estimate, and its detection skill has not been measured. The existing *Azimuthal Shear* and *Rotation Couplets* modules (with *Tilts needed* for vertical continuity) are described on the same page ({doc}`ROTATION`).
