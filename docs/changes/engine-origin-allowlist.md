- **The app's engine refuses web pages**: a page open in your browser can no longer open a connection to the engine that runs on your computer. Any page other than the app itself, and any request that names another computer, gets "403 Forbidden" before a connection is made; before, an engine started by hand (without the app's per-run key) accepted every page, and one started by the app closed the connection only after it was open. {doc}`ACCOUNTS` says what this does and does not protect.
