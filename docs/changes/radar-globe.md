- **Turn the radar network map into a globe:** the radar network map now opens as a globe you can turn with the mouse, a finger or the keyboard (drag, wheel or pinch to zoom, double-click to centre, a compass button and **N** to point north up again, **Home** to start over) and a *Globe | Flat* switch that remembers your choice and falls back to the flat map where the graphics driver has no WebGL2. The globe shows the same radars, filters, search, legend and card as the flat map and keeps the selection when you switch; zoomed in far enough it offers *Open in flat map here*. With the basemap *None* it sends nothing at all and draws land and ocean from Natural Earth outlines that ship inside the app; with OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap it asks for 64 small low-detail pictures, once. {doc}`RADAR_MAP` has the controls and what each basemap sends.
