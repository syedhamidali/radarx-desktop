- **Flash-flood indicators.** `rxd indicators` compares the rainfall of a period with flash-flood guidance, precipitation-frequency grids (return period) and the cell's own climatology, from files you supply (NetCDF, GeoTIFF or CSV; nothing is downloaded). The layers are labelled as indicators of the class of the rain they come from, carry their inputs, vintage and limits, and say that they are experimental guidance, not an official warning. The relative-to-climatology rule is the reference rule: a 36-predictor machine-learning model did not beat it in our test, and the rule itself is weak. See {doc}`HYDROLOGY`.
