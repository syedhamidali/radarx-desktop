`rxd animate --sound` adds a SYNTHESISED sound track to the storm movie (AAC, 48 kHz stereo): wind from the retrieved winds, rain from the reflectivity and thunder timed to the flashes you name, either OBSERVED GOES GLM flashes (`--flashes-from-glm`, thinned to the strongest flash of each stretch of time near the storm), a table of your own (`--flashes`) or modelled flashes (`--lightning-model`). It is not a recording: the listener is a virtual point you can place (`--sound-listener`), and the film, its tags and its record say so. The night picture no longer says it is lit by modelled lightning when no flashes are drawn.
