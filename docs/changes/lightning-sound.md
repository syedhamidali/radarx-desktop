- **Lightning sound in the viewer**: the Lightning panel can replay the flashes of the time window as sound ("Synthesised from the data, not a recording"): thunder that arrives after distance / 343 m/s (divided by the replay speed), quieter, duller and longer the farther the flash, heard from the radar or from the 3D camera with binaural-style direction, plus an optional tick at the time of each flash. A small mixer has a master level, a level and mute per layer and a limiter ceiling; the panel lists which instrument drives each layer and says that the timbre is made up. Nothing plays until you press Listen. There is no wind, rain, hail or tornado sound. See {doc}`LIGHTNING`.
