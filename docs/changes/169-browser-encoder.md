- Films are now encoded by your Google Chrome itself, inside the page that draws the frames (H.264 video and AAC sound in an MP4; VP9 and Opus in a WebM where a Chromium has no H.264 encoder). No video library is installed, downloaded or shipped any more: the movie components download of the installed app shrinks to Playwright alone (about 42 MB), and PyAV, whose wheels carry an FFmpeg with GPL parts, is only ever used when you install it yourself (`rxd animate --encoder pyav`; the new `--encoder auto|browser|pyav|png` option chooses). Stopped renders still resume, the record names the encoder and its codecs, and `--crf` keeps its meaning.
