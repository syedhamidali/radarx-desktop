# Images and third-party credits

The text of this documentation and the parts of the figures drawn by Radarx Desktop (interface, colour scales,
labels, plots) are by Radarx Desktop by LabShope Technologies. Many figures also show content from others: map
tiles, satellite imagery and terrain under the radar data, and the radar data themselves. Those parts keep their
own licences, and the credit lines below apply wherever the figures are reused.

Two of the sources have conditions worth knowing before you reuse a figure:

- **OpenStreetMap** map tiles appear in most 2D and 3D views. They need the credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
  The app shows it in the corner of the 2D map; in the 3D views it is not drawn into the picture, so it is given
  here.
- **Sentinel-2 cloudless** imagery (EOX) appears only in the figures made with *Realistic ground*. It is licensed
  for **non-commercial** use with **share-alike** terms (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), and a figure containing it is under the
  same terms.
