Methods

hybrid: Z = 300 R^1.4 test text <b>not html</b>

Grid: 100 × 100 cells of 2000 m (azimuthal-equidistant, true cell area). Rain rates (mm/h) are integrated over time with advection-corrected interpolation between scans; a gap longer than the limit is not bridged, and time without data is not counted as zero. Reflectivity in dBZ is never added or averaged as a decibel quantity.

Uncertainty model: DSD variability and calibration.

Note: No DEM: beam blockage is not evaluated.
